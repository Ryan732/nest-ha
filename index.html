Home Assistant Nest Integration

A personal, self-hosted smart home integration

This is a personal application used to connect Google Nest devices (thermostat, cameras, doorbell, and related products) to a private Home Assistant instance through the Google Smart Device Management API.

It is used solely by its owner for private home automation. It is not a commercial product, is not distributed to other users, and does not collect data for any third party.
